❓ How to Play
  • The goal is to order the numbers sequentially from left to right, top to bottom.
  • The bottom-right corner must be the empty space.
  • Multi-Slide: Click any tile in the same row or column as the empty space. All tiles between them will slide together in one move!
  • You can also use the Arrow Keys or swipe on your screen.
  • The Timer strictly starts automatically ONLY after you make your first valid move!
  • Use Hint if you get stuck to see the last moved tile.
